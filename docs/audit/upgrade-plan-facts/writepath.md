# 8. Dedup runs twice in ingest with different threshold sources

- **Compute** (`_classify_one`): `ingest.py:343` `find_similar(composed, existing, high_threshold=high_threshold)` and `ingest.py:358-360` for tombstones — always lexical Jaccard, `high_threshold` defaults to `HIGH_SIMILARITY = 0.75` (`src/bettermemory/search.py:2429`), no semantic model, no medium threshold, and the CLI never overrides it (`cli/ingest.py:167-173`). Batch-internal dedup via the `planned` fold-in (`ingest.py:429-488`).
- **Apply** (`DedupActiveGate`/`DedupTombstoneGate` via CONTENT_GATES): `_resolve_dedup_thresholds` (write.py:409-447) reads `deps.config.behavior.semantic_dedup` (default `false`, `src/bettermemory/config.py:126,361`); when on AND the embeddings extra resolves a model → cosine thresholds `semantic_high_threshold=0.85` / `semantic_medium_threshold=0.65` (config.py:159-160, 375-376); otherwise `None` → Jaccard 0.75/0.40 inside `find_similar` (search.py:2626-2629).
- So with `semantic_dedup=true` the two passes disagree in both directions (lexical-0.75 vs cosine-0.85 verdicts). **Which should win: apply-side** — it is byte-identical to the policy `memory_write` runs (the whole point of the chokepoint, write.py:585-607), and it is the one that actually blocks the durable write. # PLAN HAZARDS

1. **Threading `force` as `gc.force=True` resurrects tombstones.** `DedupTombstoneGate` checks the same `gc.force` (write.py:505-506) as `DedupActiveGate` (459-460), but ingest's contract keeps tombstone dedup ON under `--force` (ingest.py:336-341; tests/test_ingest.py:612-642; CLI help cli/ingest.py:64-71). Compute-side tombstone checking does NOT fully cover it because compute is lexical-0.75 while apply may be semantic (item 8) — a semantic-only tombstone twin would slip through. Correct shape: with `force=True`, keep `gc.force=False` and drop `DedupActiveGate` from the gates tuple instead (e.g. `tuple(g for g in CONTENT_GATES if not isinstance(g, DedupActiveGate))`).
2. **Any new `GateContext` field must have a default** or both construction sites break — `handlers/write.py:824-833` and `ingest.py:524-533` are the only two, and ingest passes every field by keyword with no `**kwargs` slack. Same for new `memory_write` params: they must be added in BOTH `handlers/write.py:785-800` and the schema-defining shim `_handlers.py:417-448`, or the MCP surface silently won't expose them.
3. **A new gate lands in `CONTENT_GATES` automatically.** `CONTENT_GATES` is derived by exclusion (`write.py:707-709`, everything except `PendingGate`), so a preference-category gate (item 5) instantly starts hard-refusing ingest rows (ingest's ack flags are all False) — auto-memory `fact`-typed files containing "I prefer …" prose would become `skip_invalid` with no override. Decide membership explicitly; extend the exclusion if ingest shouldn't inherit it. Conversely, blanket `acknowledge_scope_mismatch=True` in `_gate_context` (item 2) must not accidentally acknowledge future gates that reuse the flag.
4. **Confirm-time re-gating after `take_pending` destroys the staged write on reject.** `take_pending` pops (session.py:201-204). A gate `Reject` returned after it leaves nothing to re-confirm, and the promotion linkage (`_promotion_episodes`, session.py:159) has already been or must separately be handled — a reject must PRESERVE the source episode (mirror cancel, write.py:1094-1101, which pops linkage but keeps the episode). Either re-stage (`state.stage_write` mints a NEW pending_id — response must carry it, and promotion linkage must be re-stashed under it) or add a peek API; SessionState has none today.
5. **The original call's `force`/`acknowledge_*` flags are not in `PendingWrite`** (session.py:93-99). Re-running Credential+Dedup gates at confirm with all-False flags re-refuses writes the user already forced/acknowledged at stage time (e.g. user-inference staged with `force=True` re-trips DedupActiveGate — the store may now ALSO contain a legitimate near-twin written between stage and confirm). Persist the flags on the PendingWrite at `_stage_pending` (write.py:908) or accept the re-refusal semantics explicitly.
6. **Persisted pending payloads don't JSON-round-trip as-is.** `payload` holds `Confidence`/`Source`/`Category` **enum instances** (str-subclass — `json.dumps` works, reload yields plain `str`, which pydantic re-coerces fine) but `origin` is a pydantic `Origin` model (`origin.py:53`) — `json.dumps` raises on it. Serialize via `.value`/`model_dump()` and rebuild on load; a naive `json.dumps(pending.payload)` crashes at the first staged write with an origin.
7. **Pending writes are per-session state; a shared sidecar breaks isolation.** session.py:13-19 explicitly names cross-client confirm as the bug the `SessionRegistry` exists to prevent, and the LRU cap evicts whole states (session.py:508-510). A single `<root>/.pending_writes.jsonl` keyed by pending_id alone lets client B confirm client A's staged user-inference. Key rows by session/client or consciously document the semantics change. Also: TTL on load must mirror `_evict_expired` including the `was_recently_expired` window (session.py:281-283), or the "expired vs never existed" ValueError distinction (write.py:1025-1035) silently degrades after restart.
8. **New sidecar filename constants trip a structural test.** `tests/test_sync.py:129-219` walks every package `*_FILENAME` dotfile constant and asserts a matching pattern in `sync._GITIGNORE_LINES` (`sync.py:91-140`). A `PENDING_WRITES_FILENAME = ".pending_writes.jsonl"` fails `test_sync` until added there — and it genuinely must be gitignored: staged payloads are un-committed user content (the same privacy stance as `PROPOSALS_FILENAME`, sync.py:118-129).
9. **Any minimum-content floor above 1 stripped char breaks ≥34 handler-path test call sites** (`content="x"` via `_call(server, "memory_write", ...)` in 6 test files; plus `"y"`, single-word bodies). A proposals-style 30-char/6-token floor breaks far more. If a floor ships it must be config-gated defaulting to off, or the plan must budget the test rewrites explicitly; `_validate_write_payload` also serves `memory_update` and `accept_proposal`, so the blast radius is three tools.
10. **Converting consolidate's `propose_new` to `apply_write_gates` naively inverts two measured decisions.** The shared gates scan `payload["content"]` — if that's `body_with_provenance`, TransientGate bounces nearly every from-transcript proposal (transcript quotes carry "today i"/"we just"; consolidate.py:2278-2286) and the dedup gates false-positive sibling proposals from the same turn (Jaccard 0.882 stamped, comment 2299-2307). Scanning `proposal.body` instead makes CredentialGate miss secrets riding in the excerpt (2230-2233 requires scanning the stamped body for credentials specifically). One `GateContext.payload` cannot express "credential on stamped, transient+dedup on unstamped" — the conversion needs per-gate content or must stay hand-rolled for those gates.
