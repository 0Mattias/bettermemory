   wall.** W3-P taught that the corpus knew the words but never paired
   them, and predicted a register-matched source would pair them. The
   register-matched read now refutes the register half of that

   the registers are the anatomy's own — cooking for the food needs,
   music for the guitar need, interpersonal for the emotional one —
   and six needs still read zero. What duplicate-question structure
   encodes is PARAPHRASE: the same question in other words, a
   horizontal relation. What six of the eight needs require is
   VERTICAL: hypernym-to-instance (`guitar↔gibson`,
   `cocktail↔glass`), topic-to-ingredient (`dinner↔basil`),
   affect-to-verb (`nostalgic↔remember`). A duplicate pair about Les
   Pauls says "guitar" on both sides or "Gibson" on both sides — the
   exclusive substitution the bridge needs is structurally rare in
   title paraphrase, in any register.
2. **The two supported needs are the two paraphrase-shaped needs.**
   `battery↔charging` and `publications↔research` are horizontal
   alternations of the same topic — exactly the relation duplicate
   titles carry — and they found support across five sites, in
   whole-number counts (45, 13) that would have cleared any
   reasonable per-need floor. The mechanism works precisely where the
   relation type matches; the census now prices which needs it can

3. **The extraction infrastructure is the durable product, again.**
