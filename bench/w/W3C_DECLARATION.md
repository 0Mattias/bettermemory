# W3-C — the composition unit at the preference class: unit declaration, 2026-08-17

Fifth running unit of Lane W under `bench/W_PROGRAM_DECLARATION.md`,
third of the W3 invention branch: non-neural, no training, no fetch —
and, unlike its two parked siblings, NO NEW CORPUS BYTE. Successor to
W3-D through the composition door its record named, opening the
standing way: by declaring before building. Every input to this unit
is a derived intermediate of a prior declared unit, identified below
by sha256 from published artifacts. This document is the unit
contract — inputs, build rule, composition rule, arms, budgets,
bars — fixed before the build runs. The enforcement record is the sha
ordering: this commit (anatomy re-earned), then the bridge builder
with its CI leg, then the committed table artifact, then the reads.
A miss is published, never renegotiated.

The question W3-C asks, exactly: can the two evidence kinds the
program has measured separately — substitution structure from labeled
duplicate-title pairs (W3-P2) and stated definitional structure from
glosses, leads, and relation sections (W3-D) — composed into ONE
committed bridge table riding the rescue-expansion leg's existing
floors, connect enough ask→gold vocabulary gaps to move the
single-session-preference class, without repricing any match the
engine already makes?

Why this unit exists and why now: the two censuses decomposed the
eight anatomy needs into relation classes and priced, from opposite
sides, which structure carries which need. Their published counts
place connective evidence at five of the eight needs — coverage the
per-unit floors (each fixed at four) could not reach from either side
alone, and G1p's arithmetic needs four. The composition is the last
non-neural rung under the preference class, and this declaration is
written knowing the build floors may thin five census-supported needs
to fewer surviving bridges (§8.2) — the ladder runs anyway, because a
published PARTIAL, the first whole-question movement of this class by
any unit, is worth the declared read budget even where G1p's four is
out of reach.

## 5. The bridge — two builds and a composition, declared exactly

This repository's own committed code — w3c_bridge.py under
`bench/w/`, landing with its CI leg — deterministic end to end: same
inputs, same floors → same bytes.

- **Table P (pair substitution), W3-P §5's rule made concrete**:
  over all pairs, exclusive substitution counted symmetrically —
  token a in T1, token b in T2, a ∉ T2, b ∉ T1 (the declared
  tokenizer, carried verbatim) increments the unordered pair count
  once per pair in each qualifying position pairing a and b. Score:
  PPMI over the substitution co-occurrence table. Floors, declared
  defaults (tunable only under §6's protocol; finals in the
  artifact): substitution count ≥ 10; PPMI ≥ 2.0; mutual rank ≤ 8;
  at most 4 bridge terms per head by (PPMI descending, term
  ascending); at most 5,000 head terms by (total kept-term PPMI
  descending, head ascending).
- **Table D (definitional), W3-D §5's rule with one declared
  deviation**: per edge, the weighted attestation sum — `synonyms` 6,
  `hypernyms` and `hyponyms` 4, `gloss-link` and `lead-link` 2,
  `gloss` and `lead` 1, each distinct (label, source) attestation
  once. The deviation, stated with its reason: the edge file's
  direction is storage direction (the dump's page is the head —
  `gibson→guitar`), while the leg expands QUERY tokens and the asks
  hold the general term — so Table D is built SYMMETRIZED: an edge
  (a, b) contributes its score to head a term b AND head b term a.
  Stated synonymy is symmetric; IS-A serves expansion in both
  directions (instances for the genus, the genus for an instance).
  Floors, declared defaults (same tunability): score ≥ 2; at most 4
  bridge terms per head by (score descending, term ascending); at
  most 5,000 head terms by (total kept-term score descending, head
  ascending).
- **The composition**: for every head in either table, up to 2 terms
  from Table D in its rank order, then up to 2 from Table P in its
  rank order, duplicates keeping their first slot, then backfill
  from either table's remainder (D first) to at most 4. The D-first
  default is reasoned, not tuned: definitional terms are stated
  facts with bounded fan-out, pair terms are distributional. The
  split (2+2) is tunable only under §6's protocol; finals in the
  artifact. Heads capped at 5,000 by (present-in-both-tables first,
  then best per-table rank ascending with D before P, then head
  ascending); the emitted table source capped at 300 KB, enforced by
  trimming tail heads in that same order.
- **The artifact**: the emitted table module (the
  `SURFACE_NEIGHBORS` shape the W1 harness loads), committed under
  `bench/w/artifacts/`, beside a run JSON carrying both input
  sha256s, every floor used, the B0 rows, and the sha256 of the
  table source.

## 8. Declared confounds

1. **Incommensurable scores.** PPMI and label-weight sums share no
   scale; the composition therefore interleaves by per-table RANK,
   never by cross-table score comparison. The 2+2 split is a
   reasoned default and the declared tuning protocol is the only
   place it may move.
2. **Build-floor thinning — the named risk.** Census support at five
   needs does not survive the build floors automatically: W3-P2's
   need counts aggregate over token pairs while Table P's count
   floor binds per (a, b) TYPE — publications' 13 pairs spread
   across types may leave no type at count ≥ 10 — and Table D's
   guitar bridges must hold top-4 slots on the `guitar` head against
   every other term guitar's own gloss and relations supply. B0
   publishes exactly what survived, before any read. If the
   surviving-bridge count is under four, G1p is arithmetically out
   of reach and the unit is running for PARTIAL and for the
   measurement — declared now so the record cannot be accused of
   discovering it late.
3. **Fan-out competition at read time.** A surviving bridge must
   still out-compete the leg's other expansions inside the ranker;
   a supported build can move nothing. That outcome parks the unit
   and prices composed bridge supervision as a mechanism, which is
   precisely the question §0 asks.
4. **Additive-leg dilution.** Boost-only design bounds the blast
   radius; G2's per-type tripwires and the dev floor are the
   detectors, as in every unit since W1.
5. **Granularity.** Preference n=30; one question is 3.33 class
   points; G1p and PARTIAL both move in whole questions.
6. **Half-fitting.** G1h is the institutional answer; the even half
   tunes, the odd half testifies.
7. **The prior, registered against a three-miss record.** All three
   prior units' pre-read priors erred optimistic. This unit's,
   registered accordingly and narrowly: B0 survival at 3–4 of the
   five (battery near-certain; guitar and cocktail likely;
   publications doubtful at the default count floor; the fifth slot
   is battery's redundancy, not a new need). Gate: G1p is NOT
   predicted to hold at the default floors; the predicted honest
   outcome is W3C-PARTIAL with the class moving 2–3 whole questions,
   G2 holding, and macro@5 at or above the incumbent. A read above
   that prediction refutes the pessimistic turn; a read below it
   refutes composition itself.
