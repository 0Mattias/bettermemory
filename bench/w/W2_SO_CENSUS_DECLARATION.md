# W2 SO census — does the programming register alternate where the registers in hand do not?

The W2 body census read the labeled-duplicate corpus in hand empty on
its second surface and closed W2 entry on that corpus whole
(`bench/w/W2_BODY_CENSUS_RECORD.md`, artifact
`bench/w/results/w2-body-census-2026-08-20.json`). Its park left one
mechanical route standing and licensed nothing: the Stack Overflow
PostLinks fetch, admissible only under a declaration that argues the
fetch rather than appetites it — the body record prices the argument in
both directions and names the burden a new declaration must meet. The
owner's continuation of 2026-08-20 under the standing delegation
commissions that argument. This document carries it, and its verdict:
**the census runs.** The W2 ENTRY DECISION remains downstream — this
census decides only whether a W2 training declaration may be written
over the Stack Overflow body surface.

The sha ordering is the enforcement record, as in every unit of this
lane: this declaration first, then the reader with its CI leg, then the
register pin stage 1's fetch produces, then the runs and the record —
stage 0 runs before the fetch, at the reader's commit. A miss is
published, never renegotiated.

## 1. The argument, both ways, and the verdict

FOR — three legs, each mechanical:

- **The presence gradient.** In the corpus in hand the prize vocabulary
  is borrowed, not native: across the kept pair bodies the presence
  rows put boolean in 5, exception in 233, error in 3,292, toggle in
  150 and flag in 190
  (`bench/w/results/w2-body-census-2026-08-20.json`). Stack Overflow is
  the one pinned corpus where these words are the working vocabulary of
  the register rather than terms imported into it.

- **The crossing gradient.** Every exclusive crossing the in-hand
  corpus produced sits in its consumer-tech register — error/exception
  4 of 4, undo/rollback 1 of 1, revert/rollback 1 of 1, with that
  register holding 22,950 of the 37,502 kept pairs (same artifact). The
  register also holds most of the pair mass, so this is direction, not
  proof — but the direction is uniform: what little crossing the corpus
  shows lives closest to the programming register, and this census
  reads the limit of that gradient.

- **The mechanism the in-hand corpus cannot exhibit.** The body
  record's surviving mechanism is register reuse: askers of one need
  name a shared referent by its one name on both sides of an edge, and
  the cross-form mate, where it appears, is the same author's
  elaboration, never a paraphrase's alternative. Every edge in hand
  joins two askers drawn from one population. Stack Overflow's
  duplicate graph differs structurally in exactly the dimension under
  test: its curation culture closes surface-phrased need statements
  into canonical implementation-phrased questions, and the committed
  six are surface↔implementation pairs almost to a row. If cross-form
  alternation exists anywhere in a labeled pair corpus, it exists
  across that closure boundary — and the imported prose rule sharpens
  the test precisely there: pasted stack traces and code, the cheap
  route for implementation vocabulary to leak into a surface-phrased
  body, sit in code and pre spans and fall to the cleaning fixpoint.

AGAINST — the body record's objection restated at full strength, plus
one this declaration adds:

- Register reuse survived a presence check with fat marginals, and
  referent anchoring does not care what population an asker belongs
  to: two askers hitting the same exception both name it by the string
  in front of them — and on Stack Overflow the string in front of them
  is more uniform, not less.

- The surface↔implementation split may live inside bodies rather than
  across edges. error/exception in hand reads 4 exclusive against 22
  same-side (`bench/w/results/w2-body-census-2026-08-20.json`) —
  co-occurring, never substituting — and same-side is exactly the raw
  material pair-positive supervision cannot use. A register richer in
  both forms can simply be richer in both forms per body.

- **The volume null.** At the rates the in-hand tech register realized,
  scale alone lifts error/exception past the support floor while
  leaving at zero every pair that read zero: a corpus can be enormous
  and still empty where the prize is. The title census taught that
  shape; this census must not mistake volume for mechanism.

THE VERDICT — the census runs, because the bands discriminate the
mechanisms and every outcome retires a question. PARK is the volume
null's own prediction: register reuse holds even in the register where
the prize vocabulary is native, and W2-on-labeled-duplicates closes
everywhere it has been priced. TWITCH means at least one pair beyond
volume's reach crossed. LICENSE means the class exists where the
argument said it must, and the mechanism program has its first live
supervision source. One standing consideration weights running over
rejecting, and it is the program's, not the corpus's: the interim
sentence — the bars unreached by our own means, published as such —
currently stands on a corpus priced whole with one named door
unpriced. Priced empty, the sentence closes over the largest labeled
duplicate corpus in the one register where the prize vocabulary is
native. Priced non-empty, the only live route to an own-trained
shippable artifact opens. Either reading is worth a fetch smaller than
most of the per-site archives already pinned.

## 2. Stage 0 — the Stack Overflow surface already in hand, read first

W3-P's reader derived, from the pinned Stack Overflow Posts archive,
the legacy closure-notice title pairs: 40,103 pairs, the closed
question's own title in the left column and the canonical target's in
the right, the derived file's sha256 recorded in
`bench/w/results/w3p-census-2026-08-17.json`. That file is the only
Stack Overflow pair surface in hand, and it is read before any fetch:
the committed six, the expanded family and the presence rows, engine
stem space, title surface, through the same imported counting the body
census ran — under the site label `stackoverflow-legacy-titles` — plus
the directional row of §4, which the file's column order supports. On
a title surface the markup-text variant is degenerate (the two
surfaces coincide) and its columns are published as they read.

Stage 0 gates nothing, in either direction, and the declaration says
why before the count exists: the surface is title-thin — the in-hand
title census showed what thin marginals do to a joint event — and the
legacy closure convention marks an early era of the site, not a sample
of its duplicate graph. Its evidentiary value is asymmetric: crossings
here would be strong evidence the register alternates; zeros here add
almost nothing. It runs because it is in hand, free, and completes the
record. Artifact: `bench/w/results/w2-so-stage0-<date>.json`. The
input's sha256 is re-verified against the recorded value before the
read; a mismatch stops the unit.

## 3. Stage 1 — the fetch, named in plain language

What is fetched: one file, `stackoverflow.com-PostLinks.7z`, a member
of the archive.org `stackexchange` item — the same publisher item every
Stack Exchange pin in `bench/w/corpora.json` names — from
https://archive.org/download/stackexchange/stackoverflow.com-PostLinks.7z,
under a fetch cap of 200 MB (the publisher's metadata declares the
member well under it). Verification on arrival, before the pin: the
publisher's declared md5 for the member is recomputed over the fetched
bytes and required equal; the sha256 over the exact bytes is computed
and recorded. The pin: a new register entry,
`stackoverflow-postlinks-archive`, with source URL, retrieval date,
byte count, sha256, the publisher md5, and the license line the Posts
pin carries (CC BY-SA, Stack Exchange network content, per-post
attribution obligations noted for any redistribution). The pin lands in
`bench/w/corpora.json` before stage 2 reads a byte. The fetched bytes
live beside the Posts archive outside the repo; nothing fetched is
committed. The archive is expected to carry `PostLinks.xml`; a missing
member, a cap overrun, or a failed check is a published park of the
whole unit — there is one site, so the family's per-site
missing-member rule degenerates to a stop.

## 4. Stage 2 — the body census over the duplicate graph

Input: the two Stack Overflow pins — `stackoverflow-posts-archive` (in
hand; its member is `Posts.xml`) and stage 1's
`stackoverflow-postlinks-archive` — each sha256 re-verified over the
exact bytes before any member is read, a mismatch stopping the unit.
The one structural difference from the body census is the publisher's
layout: the network's largest site ships as per-table archives, so
`PostLinks.xml` and `Posts.xml` stream from two archives rather than
one. Everything that counts is imported byte-for-byte from committed
modules: the edge rule (`w3p2_pairs.duplicate_edges` — LinkTypeId 3,
document order, exact repeats dropped), the body surfaces
(`w2_body_census.body_surfaces` — the body declaration's §2 cleaning
fixpoint, unchanged), the pair-keep rule, the probe sets
(`w2_body_census.committed_probe_pairs`), exclusive substitution
(`w3p_pairs.need_supported`), the presence rows, the same-side row,
the NEEDS continuity row, and the ladder constants.

The arrangement — the one piece of new plumbing, declared with its
reason: the reference reader caches every wanted body and resolves
edges afterward; this table is of a different order (24,101,803
question rows in the pinned archive —
`bench/w/results/w3p-census-2026-08-17.json`), so this reader resolves
eagerly instead. The edge pass produces, per wanted post id, a
reference count and its pending edges in edge order; the body pass
caches a wanted question row's body on arrival and resolves,
immediately and in edge order, every pending edge whose other member
is already cached; a resolved edge's members leave the cache when
their counts reach zero; edges unresolved when the stream ends are the
unresolved count. The census counts are order-free sums, so the
arrangement can change no number — and the CI leg proves it: on the
committed synthetic fixture the eager reader and the imported
reference (`w2_body_census.W2BodyCensus.add_site`) produce an
identical counts block and the same derived rows as sets, with the
fixture exercising a self-edge, a shared-target fan, an unresolved
edge and a body-less member.

The derived file: `bench/w/corpus/derived/w2-so-bodies-<date>.tsv`,
one line per kept pair, seven columns — site, post id, related id,
left prose, right prose, left markup-text, right markup-text — not
committed (CC BY-SA per-post attribution), its sha256 recorded in
every artifact that reads it. The census stage re-run from this file
must reproduce the counts block identically, directional and
unique-target rows included; that repeat is the declared determinism
check.

Two informational rows this declaration owns, neither gating:

- **THE DIRECTIONAL ROW.** Per probe pair (a, b), among its exclusive
  crossings: the count with a's stem on the PostId side, and the count
  with a's stem on the RelatedPostId side. PostId is the closed
  question, RelatedPostId its closure target; the mechanism of §1
  predicts surface forms concentrate on the closed side, and the row
  is counted neutrally so the record can read the prediction rather
  than assume it. Consistency is pinned twice: the two direction
  counts must sum to the imported rule's exclusive count at run time,
  and the CI leg asserts the same identity on the fixture.

- **THE UNIQUE-TARGET ROW.** Per probe pair, the count of distinct
  RelatedPostId values among its exclusive crossings. On this corpus
  one canonical question absorbs many phrasings, so five crossings
  minted by one target and five minted by five are different evidence;
  the record's headline for any supported row carries its
  unique-target count beside it. The reading is pre-committed here;
  the gate arithmetic is untouched.

The tech-register column the imported reader publishes is vestigial on
a single-register corpus and reads zero by construction; this sentence
is its documentation.

## Declared confounds

1. **Volume and mechanism are entangled at a scale-blind support
   floor.** Five exclusive crossings mean different things in a
   thousand pairs and in a million; the ladder's constants are kept
   for family comparability, and the band-meaning paragraph of §5 is
   the declared correction: rates and unique-target counts are read
   beside every supported cell.
2. **Mega-target minting.** One canonical question can mint a probe
   pair's entire support; the unique-target row exists so the record
   cannot present concentrated support as distributed support.
3. **Stage 0's era bias.** The legacy closure convention marks an
   early era of the site; its pairs are not a sample of today's
   duplicate graph, which is why stage 0 gates nothing.
4. **Bare-prose pastes.** The fixpoint drops implementation text in
   code and pre spans, but an error string pasted as bare prose stays
   on the surface and, sitting on the surface-phrased side, destroys
   exclusivity for that pair. The bias runs against support — a
   supported read survives it; a park must name it among the candidate
   mechanisms rather than treat the zero as pure register reuse.
5. **Direction is structural, not causal.** PostId→RelatedPostId is
   closure direction, usually but not always newer→older; the
   directional row reads sides, not ages or causes.
6. **The probe sets are table-derived.** As in every census of this
   family: they measure the relations the curated table carries, which
   three instruments have made the named prize — not every relation
   that matters.
7. **Stemming can manufacture or destroy matches.** The engine stemmer
   is used because every downstream consumer matches in its space; a
   pair the stemmer conflates is reported as conflated rather than
   silently counted.
