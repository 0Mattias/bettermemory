## The zero is real — the validity check, recorded

A zero this absolute earns the same suspicion a competitor's zero
earned, so presence was checked before the number was written down:
the probe vocabulary IS in the corpus and the pipeline reads it. In
stem space, counting titles containing each probe stem on either
side:

```
toggle 31   flag 20   undo 40   rollback 7   revert 45
timeout 20  expiry 20  boolean 0  error 476  exception 1
(reproduce: stem each title's content_tokens with the engine
 stemmer and count membership over the committed TSV)
```

The words appear; they do not substitute across duplicate edges. Two
mechanisms, both honest: people asking the same question in this
register reuse the same technical noun rather than its cross-form
mate, and the marginals are thin — vocabulary present in tens of
titles out of tens of thousands leaves the joint exclusive event
almost no room to occur even in principle. Both point the same way:
short duplicate-question TITLES in the general-tech register are the
wrong surface for the prize class, however many of them there are.

## What the park opens — named, not licensed

A new data AXIS is a new declaration's burden, and two candidates
are named here so it argues against the record rather than from
appetite:

1. **Bodies, not titles.** The pinned per-site archives carry full
   `Posts.xml` bodies; a duplicate pair's bodies are long-form
   paraphrases of one need, and the marginals objection above is
   about title length, not about the corpus. Unread, unpriced,
   pinned — the obvious first candidate.
2. **The programming register.** Stack Overflow's own duplicate
   edges remain unpinned; the twitch band did not license the fetch
   and this record does not either. A declaration arguing for it
   must first explain why register would change what registers in
   hand did not.

Both are owner-door material now: with every mechanically available
route measured, the next unit is a commission, not a continuation —
and the standing alternative is the one the interim sentence has
carried all along: the bars restate to what own-built machinery
honestly reaches, and the published truth stays exactly as measured.
