# W2 pair-class census — does the labeled-pair corpus carry the prize?

Three units have now measured the substitutability family shut on
instruments that can see: static tables do no detectable work at the
rebuilt instrument's scale (`bench/retrieval/I1_RECORD.md`), two-pass
feedback requery closed with its oracle under half its bar
(`bench/REQUERY_CENSUS_2_DECLARATION.md`'s census), and window width
moved the cross-form families backwards at triple the updates
(`bench/w/W1C_RECORD.md`). What remains is specified by elimination:
a from-scratch contrastive objective over labeled pairs, whose
training signal must reward CO-OCCURRING pairs that never substitute.
This census prices that route's PREMISE before any training byte: does
the labeled-pair corpus already in hand actually contain the classes
such an objective would need to learn from? The W2 ENTRY DECISION
remains an owner door; this census only decides whether a W2 training
declaration may be written.

## 1. The input — in hand, pinned, no fetch

`bench/w/corpus/derived/w3p2-pairs-2026-08-17.tsv` — the labeled
duplicate-question pairs W3-P2 extracted from the pinned per-site
archives (site, left title, right title; provenance in
`bench/w/W3P2_DECLARATION.md` §3). The census artifact records the
file's sha256, its line count, and the per-site distribution; the
consumer-tech register subset (superuser + apple + android) is
reported beside the full file in every cell. The Stack Overflow
PostLinks archive is NOT pinned and is NOT fetched by this census —
by the inventory's own order-of-operations, the in-hand register runs
first, and the fetch is a named follow-up only where the ladder below
says so.

## 2. The reads — W3-P2's Stage-0 rule, reused verbatim

The counting rule is `bench/w/W3P2_DECLARATION.md` §4's exclusive
substitution, unchanged: for a probe pair (a, b), count labeled pairs
(T1, T2) where a's stem appears among one title's stemmed tokens, b's
stem among the other's, and neither stem in the opposite title —
either direction. Stemming is the engine's own `_stem_token`, the
same space every table and every probe in this program matches in.
Same-side co-occurrence (both stems in one title) is reported beside
it, informationally — it is weaker evidence but some objectives can
use it.

The probe sets, derived rather than chosen:

- **THE COMMITTED SIX** — the dev instrument's named cross-form
  bridges, byte for byte from `bench/w/w1b_geometry_probe.py`
  (`CROSS_FORM`). This is the prize class: the pairs three units
  measured unreachable by substitutability objectives. The license
  reads on this set alone.
- **THE EXPANDED FAMILY** — the pairs enumerated mechanically from
  the hand table by `bench/w/w1c_geometry_census.py::expanded_cross_form`,
  imported, not copied. Reported in full (per-pair counts and the
  support distribution), gating nothing.
- **THE PREFERENCE CONTINUITY ROW** — the eight `NEEDS` entries of
  W3-P2's own census, recomputed by the same code path so the two
  censuses read side by side without renegotiation. Informational:
  the preference class's route was measured shut at W3-C and this
  census does not reopen it; the row exists because the W2 owner
  decision will want both classes on one page.

## 3. The criterion, stated before any count exists

Support means what W3-P2's evidentiary minimum meant, and the ladder
reads on the FULL file (training is free to use every register; the
tech-register subset is reported beside it):

```
SUPPORTED   a probe pair with >= 5 exclusive-substitution pairs
LICENSE     >= 4 of the committed six SUPPORTED
            -> writing the W2 training declaration is licensed
TWITCH      2 or 3 of the committed six SUPPORTED
            -> the named follow-up is the Stack Overflow PostLinks
               fetch (the inventory's scale question), a census
               re-read on the enlarged corpus, and nothing else
PARK        0 or 1 of the committed six SUPPORTED
            -> the labeled-duplicate route cannot carry the prize
               class; W2-entry-on-this-data closes, the census is
               the record, and what remains is a different data
               axis (the definitional-bridge route W3-P2 §9 named,
               or a corpus class a new declaration must argue for)
```

Derivation, arithmetic not appetite: a contrastive objective can only
learn a bridge that appears in its positives; a prize pair with zero
exclusive substitutions is invisible to any loss over this corpus,
and one with fewer than five is below the evidentiary minimum every
census in this family has used. Four of six is the smallest majority
that leaves the objective a class to learn rather than a pair to
memorize. The TWITCH band exists because the inventory names Stack
Overflow as the largest missing register of exactly this vocabulary
— partial support in the general-tech registers is the one outcome a
programming-register corpus could plausibly overturn.

## 4. Constraint ledger

- Read-only: the census reads the committed TSV, the committed probe
  modules, and the engine's public stemmer. No engine code changes;
  nothing is trained; nothing under `bench/longmemeval/`,
  `bench/msc/` or `bench/heldout/` is touched.
- No fetch: the PostLinks archive is not downloaded under this
  declaration, whatever the outcome — the TWITCH row licenses a
  follow-up declaration, not a download tonight.
- Deterministic artifact: sorted iteration, lexicographic ties, no
  wall-clock content beyond the provenance date.
- No changelog entry.

## Declared confounds

1. **Titles are not memories.** Duplicate-question titles are short,
   interrogative, and register-skewed; a bridge present here may
   still be rare in conversational stores, and one absent here may
   exist in bodies the extraction never read. The census prices the
   corpus W2 would actually train on, nothing more.
2. **The probe sets are table-derived.** As in W1c: they measure the
   relations the curated table carries, which three instruments have
   made the named prize — not every relation that matters.
3. **Stemming can manufacture or destroy matches.** The engine
   stemmer is used because every downstream consumer matches in its
   space; a pair the stemmer conflates is reported as conflated
   rather than silently counted.

## What is not claimed

- Not a statement about Stack Overflow's registers — that corpus is
